About Yogee

From College Friends to Sweet Dreams - Our Journey

Our Sweet Beginning

Courtney, Mike, Gia, Inod, Rhiye and Gio met in college, bonding over a shared love for yogurt. What started as a simple connection soon evolved into a deep friendship—and the idea of turning their passions into a business.

After graduating with degrees in business, they took the leap and pooled their savings together to open their first yogurt franchise, Yogee, in Calamba, Laguna.

The menu featured creative innovations like the "Perfect Swirl" and "Buttercup", alongside signature matcha blends and croffles. Their unique concept quickly gained traction, and Yogee became a local favorite.

Through hard work and dedication, they transformed their shared love for yogurt into a thriving business. Their journey proves that great ideas often begin with the simplest passions—and sometimes, the sweetest dreams are born over a cup of yogurt™.
